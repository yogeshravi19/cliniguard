Fusion Architecture Formulas

CLINIGUARD runs a single-pass inference that extracts 4 distinct NLP signals and combines them using a task-conditional fusion model.

🔵 Signal 1 — Med-ISP (Medical Info State Probe)

Med-ISP = 1.0 - min(drug_term_hits / (word_count * 0.05), 1.0)

Ensures clinical safety by verifying that responses include necessary therapeutic drug terminology.

🟠 Signal 2 — C-AAS (Clinical Attention Alignment)

C-AAS = 1.0 - min(context_term_hits / (word_count * 0.04), 1.0)

Measures focus on clinical context terms (e.g. allergies, weight, patient history) to prevent omission errors.

🔴 Signal 3 — Med-EEM (Entropy-Energy Monitor)

p = uncertain_word_hits / word_count
H(p) = -p*log2(p) - (1-p)*log2(1-p)
Med-EEM = min(H(p) * (1 + p), 1.0)

Binary Shannon Entropy calculation of uncertainty. High density of hedging words ("maybe", "could be") increases entropy and risk score.

🟡 Signal 4 — CDT (Clinical Drift Tracker)

CDT = 1.0 - CosineSimilarity(vector_q, vector_a)

Tracks topical drift. High cosine similarity between the frequency vectors of Question (Q) and Answer (A) yields low drift risk.

Task-Conditional Weights

Different clinical tasks require different prioritization. For instance, allergy checking prioritizes context attendance (C-AAS), while drug dosing prioritizes precision / certainty (Med-EEM).

Task Type Med-ISP (α) C-AAS (β) Med-EEM (γ) CDT (δ) Primary Risk Factor
Drug Dosing 0.20 0.20 0.45 0.15 Hedging, vagueness, or uncertain dosing instruction
Allergy Checking 0.15 0.50 0.20 0.15 Failure to attend to documented patient allergies
General Diagnosis 0.20 0.15 0.15 0.50 Drifting off-topic or answering unrelated query

Research Overview

This panel summarizes the datasets, models, and evaluation metrics that power the CLINIGUARD system.

Datasets Used

  • MedHall Bench – medhall_bench.parquet (clinical Q&A benchmark)
  • Additional curated clinical QA datasets (e.g., MedQA, PubMedQA) – integrated during training

Trained Models

  • Unified Fusion Model (LLM + 4 signal fusion)
  • Task‑conditional heads for Drug Dosing, Allergy Checking, and General Diagnosis

Evaluation Metrics & Formulas

Risk scores are computed from the four signal formulas (Med‑ISP, C‑AAS, Med‑EEM, CDT) and combined using the task‑conditional weights shown in the Insights tab.
